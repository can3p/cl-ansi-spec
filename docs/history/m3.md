## What was built

- **Declarations and registry** (`src/model/define-node.lisp`,
  `nodes-*.lisp`): `define-node` with typed fields, enums, target kinds,
  docstrings and examples; every node kind declared by area (structure,
  entries, blocks, tables, inlines, glossary, bnf, math); `kind` reserved
  for the node kind, an entry's kind is `entry-kind`; the root is
  `:document`.  A documentation ratchet per area
  (`tests/model/undocumented-<area>.sexp`) ended empty.
- **Ownership table** (`src/model/owners.sexp`): every vocabulary key and
  event kind has exactly one owner step and handler file; a test checks it.
  `tests/model/nodes.lisp` maps each owned key to the kinds its handler
  builds.
- **Builder core** (`src/model/build.lisp`): cursor over a job's events,
  handler table keyed on (event type, key), container stack, spans joined
  from begin and end, unhandled events collected in the build report,
  `build-job`, `build-model`, `build-fixture`; `make model FILE= LINES=`.
  3.16 added an end-of-input hook, the container end span for on-close and
  `chapter-jobs` in `src/source.lisp`.
- **Builders per area**: structure with section numbers counted and checked
  against TeX's number and the `\DefineSection` write (3.8); dictionary
  entries and every signature macro (3.9); blocks and the list of dropped
  presentational events, each with a reason (3.10); tables and figures,
  figure numbers from captions (3.11); text and fonts, ligatures and
  character codes per font (3.12); semantic inlines (3.13); glossary
  (3.14); BNF (3.15); math lists with character tables per family (3.15m).
- **Ids** (3.16): chapter label, `\DefineSection` label, first entry name
  with `<name>/<ftype>` on collision, glossary term, `<parent-id>/<n>` for
  sections without a label, `<chapter-id>/fig-<n>` for unlabelled figures.
  Remaining collisions are `:id-collision` problems.
- **References**: section, chapter and figure references through the
  labels of the events, with `:aliases` kept on chapter and section nodes,
  table of contents and list of figures (3.17, 3.19b); operator references
  by name, the wrapper's kind preferred on a shared name (3.18); term
  references with stated inflection rules and tiers, irregulars as data
  with a reason each, and `:ambiguous-term`, `:unresolved-term`,
  `:missing-sense` problems (3.19).  The pinned list of unresolved terms is
  `tests/model/unresolved-terms.sexp`.
- **Exports**: S-expression and JSON writers over the registry with the
  omission rules and file layout (3.7); `cl-ansi-spec/json` (3.U1) and
  `cl-ansi-spec/sha256` (3.U2); the JSON Schema generator (3.4) and the
  reference-doc generator (3.5), both under `make generate` through the
  `define-generator` registry; the validator of the schema subset with JSON
  pointers (3.6); `make export` with `manifest.json` (patch list) and
  `report.json` (3.20); `make test` validates every exported file, and the
  export now passes the schema with no pinned violation (3.20v).
- **External validator** (3.21): a CI step validates `build/export/` with
  ajv-cli (CI only, #50); the Python check-jsonschema was too slow.
- **Schema audit fixes** (3.23): the milestone-close audit of
  `docs/model-reference.md` against the whole export found references
  left unresolved without a report (`:next-figure`, `:this-section`),
  raw TeX in an `angle-name`, and declarations that said what the data
  does not; 3.23 fixed them.  (Coordinator: complete with its PR number
  and outcome.)
- **Sample export and consumer guide** (3.22): `docs/consuming.md` with the
  worked example on `endp`, `make sample`, validated by the suite.
- **Patches found on the way**: 0007–0009 (3.16p), 0010–0023 (term typos
  and misused `\term`, 3.19p), 0024 (quadrant diagram as a code block).

## What turned out wrong

- The whole export broke the schema in 63 places (54 `math-char` nodes
  without a span, 8 `figure` and 1 `glossary-example` under parents the
  declaration did not allow), found only when 3.20 validated the whole
  export, not by the per-area fixtures.  They were pinned in
  `*export-schema-violations*`, then fixed in 3.20v by giving nodes what
  their declaration requires or admitting the parent the sources use; none
  was dropped.
- Ownership moved from the plan: `:input` and `:misc` went to 3.13, not 3.8
  and 3.11, once the vocabulary showed what they are; `:write` has one
  owner, 3.8.  3.2's shapes lacked fields the math events carry (`limits`,
  `thickness`, `number-left`), added in 3.15m.
- Builder names clashed when the parallel pull requests merged
  (`strip-spans`, `problem-messages`, `spec-nodes`): renamed in merge
  fixes.  A fixture of 3.13 broke once 3.9 built the entry.
- Chapter and section nodes lacked their other labels, so 41 section
  references stayed unresolved after 3.17; 3.19b added `:aliases`, cleaned
  `\fi` and empty `\misc` texts and set `\f{…}` as a `:code` (unresolved
  operator references 309 → 89).
- A table inside a table cell (concept-numbers.tex:580) was not held by
  the builder: a cell holds inlines, and a table there would change the
  shape every consumer reads.  It was a drawing, patched in 0024.
- Source errors fixed by patches, not code: run-in `\code`s (0007), an
  empty `\code` (0008), a duplicated `\DefineSection` label (0009), 12
  term typos and 20 misused `\term` (0010–0023).
- Two entries of dict-conses.tex end inside their last paragraph (:201,
  :4021); the core reported `:unclosed` and `:underflow` until 3.9 handled
  them.
- The schema audit ran only at the milestone's close, and found model
  defects, not just wording (3.23): an audit of a builder's declarations
  against real data belongs in the builder's own step (the `lisp-review`
  checklist now asks builder tests to validate their nodes).

## Left out on purpose

- Notes keep their TeX text: the body typesets nothing while the options
  hide notes, and emitting the content would be a semantic-layer change
  (decided in 3.16, "the editors' text, not the standard's").
- Nine `:not-a-term` rows stay pinned: the intended markup was not plain.
  The glossary sub-sense rows stay pinned too.
- No post-pass folding flat list records in 3.10, no part-of-speech check
  in term resolution, no span field in node declarations (every node has
  `node-span`), no multi-paragraph notes.
- No normalisation table in code for `\ftype` variants and no code
  exception for a typo'd reference: patches.
- cmex has the delimiters, big operators and radicals; other slots are
  reported.

## Options weighed

- Section numbers: counted by the builder and checked against TeX's own
  number, versus read only from the writes (only 278 sections have a
  `\DefineSection`).
- Sections without a label: `<parent-id>/<n>`, stable because the parent's
  id is, never a link target from the sources.
- Math as a small tree of its own with Unicode characters, versus
  typesetting.
- Which model sets what: opus for 3.0, 3.1, 3.2, 3.3, 3.16, 3.19 and the
  3.20v declarations; math split off as 3.15m with a full critic; 3.12
  moved to wave B so builders could assert real text.
- Whole-milestone critic (sonnet), all accepted: patches in pristine
  coordinates (M4); the ownership table; text before builders; M4's 4.3
  and 4.4 before wave C; subsections, one ratchet per area and
  regenerate-on-merge for shared files; tiering; 3.16p reserved for
  patches; unchecked sections and expected id collisions listed in 3.16.
- 3.2's critic: anchors are blocks too; the setf half of
  `\DefineMultiAccessorWithValues`-style macros is a second signature;
  glossary parts of speech and usages are lists; BNF kinds prefixed
  `bnf-`.  3.19's critic: per-rule and per-tier use counts pinned, the
  reasons `:variant` and `:compound` added.
